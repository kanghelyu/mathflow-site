Two results worth stating on their own

Both are exact, and both are the reason the rest of the reconstruction hangs together.

factorisation of D(u)
D(u) = ∏_i ( 1 + u·t^(N-1)·C_i )  =  1 + u·D_1 + u²·D_2   (N = 2)
The generating function of the Macdonald operators is a product of linear factors in the Cherednik operators. Matching the coefficients of u and u² gives t(C_1+C_2) = D_1 and t²C_1C_2 = D_2. Commutativity of the D_k is therefore inherited from [C_1, C_2] = 0 rather than proved a second time.

Notation trap — the same letter for two different operators

Section 6.2.3 and eq. (52) of the paper both write C_i, but eq. (52) silently redefines C_i := x_i · C̃_i. At N = 2 the discrepancy is

x_1·C̃_1 − C_1 = (1/t − 1)·s_{1,2}·q^{D_1}

and it does not vanish on symmetric functions — so this is a genuine change of operator, not a harmless abuse of notation. Every label in the verification harness names which of the two objects it means.

Method note: symbolic simplification alone is unreliable once s_{1,2} appears. Identities were accepted only after cancel followed by substitution at three independent rational points. Three points is evidence, not proof — wherever a purely symbolic route existed, it was used instead. Operator powers are iterated k times; a scalar power produces false failures.
