What this is

Every map on this site is produced with OmniFlow, a universal flow-map tool: cards plus typed relations. The published pages are a static snapshot of a local OmniFlow vault, rendered by the unmodified OmniFlow Studio client running against the snapshot instead of a server.

Card notes carry the mathematics and the evidence; labels are kept as plain text so they read correctly at every zoom level.

Reading a map

  • Arrows point from what provides to what results.
  • Solid = derivation or flow · dashed = citation.
  • Colour encodes card type: definition, theorem, lemma, task.
  • Click a card for its note; click again to highlight its neighbourhood.
